Project Background
This was my first public project coming into Unreal. To challenge myself, I chose to replicate the gameplay of Dark Souls by making a boss with complex AI behavior and fluid player controls. Through this project, I learned the ins and outs of the engine, gaining experience in blueprints, C++, behavior trees, and Niagara.
AI Behavior
The hardest part of making the boss AI was making sure it used the right attack in the right situation. Upon research, I found out that Dark Souls uses a "Combat Wheel" that helps determine what attack to execute based on the player's state.

With this information, I made my own version of the "Combat Wheel" using the distance from the player as a variable to determine the abilty to execute. To drive the AI behavior, I used a behavior tree to both visualize and fine-tune values.

I created a behavior tree task that contains an array of all available attacks and a numeric weight attached to it. When the AI controller executes the task, it goes through the array and utilizes the weights to randomly choose an ability.

As a result, if the enemy AI happens to be far away from the player, it has a higher chance of executing a gap-closer ability. If the enemy is close to the player, then they are more likely to perform close-ranged combo attacks.
Player Mechanics
Player attacks are stored in data assets, where damage, animation, input values, and general information is stored to easily implement combo attacks. This system would be modified to be used in future projects like SPAR-K-RUMBLE.
